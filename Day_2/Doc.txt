## 🧩 Step 2: Define the v0 User Flow (Write This Down)

This is the **core of your product**.

### User Flow v0

1. User enters:

   * Topic (e.g. *Agentic AI in marketing*)
   * Optional tone (default: professional)
2. User clicks **Generate**
3. BlueDraft Studio:

   * Thinks through the topic
   * Suggests key angles
   * Drafts a LinkedIn-ready post
4. User:

   * Edits manually
   * Copies the content

⚠️ No login.
⚠️ No saving posts.
⚠️ No publishing.

Simple = powerful.

## 🧠 Step 3: Decide the 2 Models (You Mentioned This)

You said: *“with 2 models”* — perfect.

### Model 1: **Research Brain**

Purpose:

* Understand topic
* Identify key points
* Avoid hallucination-heavy fluff

Example role prompt:

> “You are a research-focused AI agent.
> Your job is to think deeply, structure ideas, and highlight important points — not to write marketing copy.”

---

### Model 2: **Drafting Brain**

Purpose:

* Turn research into a **LinkedIn-native post**
* Clear structure, good hook, readable flow

Example role prompt:

> “You are a LinkedIn content strategist.
> Write concise, thoughtful posts with a strong opening, clear paragraphs, and a reflective ending.”

⚠️ Today you **only define roles**, not code them.
